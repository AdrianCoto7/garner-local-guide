OUTDOOR ADVENTURES

Lake Benson Park

Lake Benson Park is a great place for families to relax and enjoy the outdoors. The park offers open green spaces, walking trails, playgrounds, and picnic areas.

Things to Do

  • Explore 1.8 miles of walking trails.
  • Enjoy a family picnic.
  • Visit the Garner Veterans Memorial.
  • Spend time at the playgrounds.
  • Bring your dog to the dog park.

📍 Address:
921 Buffaloe Road, Garner, NC

🕒 Hours:
Sunrise to sunset.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Best for:
Family picnics, walking, and relaxing.

SPORTS & RECREATION

Garner Recreational Park

Garner Recreational Park is a great destination for families who enjoy sports and outdoor activities. Visitors can play, exercise, and spend quality time together.

Things to Do

  • Play baseball on the athletic fields.
  • Explore 2.25 miles of mountain biking trails.
  • Enjoy the playground.
  • Visit the North Garner Dog Park.
  • Have lunch at the picnic shelter.

📍 Address:
221 East Garner Road, Garner, NC

🕒 Hours:
Sunrise to sunset.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Best for:
Sports, outdoor recreation, and active families.

NATURE & WILDLIFE

White Deer Park

White Deer Park is a wonderful location for families who enjoy nature and learning. The park offers scenic trails, educational experiences, and opportunities to explore wildlife.

Things to Do

  • Explore two miles of paved walking trails.
  • Visit the Nature Center.
  • Discover the wildflower meadow.
  • Enjoy a picnic with friends and family.
  • Learn about local animals and plants.

📍 Address:
2400 Aversboro Road, Garner, NC

🕒 Hours:
Sunrise to sunset.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Best for:
Nature lovers and families with children.
